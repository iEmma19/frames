Community Rules
01

Freedom of Expression

Feel free to chat and express yourself. All languages are allowed.

02

Mutual Respect

Personal insults, excessively vulgar language, constant provocation, or harassment will not be tolerated.

03

Respect for the Staff

All staff members must be treated with respect. Disrespect or undermining staff may result in a ban.

04

Transparency

Bans longer than 10 hours must be accompanied by screenshot evidence. 1-hour bans are exempt.

05

Staff Impartiality

Banning for personal reasons is strictly prohibited. Punishments must be fair and impartial.
